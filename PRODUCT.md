# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Students, educators, travellers, and culturally curious visitors exploring major monuments of the ancient Greek world. They need a fast geographic overview, bilingual context, and trustworthy source links without creating an account.

## Product Purpose

Present 37 ancient Greek wonders on an interactive map that works as a static GitHub Pages site. Success means a visitor can locate, search, filter, compare, and read about every entry in English or Greek on desktop or mobile.

## Positioning

A focused bilingual atlas that connects the monuments of the ancient Greek world to their geography without hiding the map behind a content portal.

## Brand Personality

Scholarly, monumental, restrained. The interface should feel editorial and archaeological while remaining a practical mapping tool.

## Anti-references

No tourist-brochure gloss, fake antiquity textures, ornamental Greek-key overload, generic dashboard cards, AI-generated imagery, or unlabeled reconstructions.

## Design Principles

- Keep the map primary and controls secondary.
- Distinguish surviving sites, ruins, and lost monuments without implying false certainty.
- Keep English and Greek equally complete.
- Make provenance visible through source links and concise methodology notes.
- Use progressive disclosure so the initial map remains fast and calm.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA where practical, preserve keyboard navigation and visible focus, avoid color-only meaning, respect reduced motion, and keep the complete experience usable at 320 CSS pixels.
